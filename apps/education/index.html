Validation boundary

Accreditation and licensing are different checks. Accreditation is a voluntary quality designation; a state license is the legal authorization to operate. Always validate both at the individual location level.

External directories remain the source of truth. LightkeeperIQ provides direct access and workflow support but does not certify a center's current standing.

About the intelligence

Marketplace context, responsibly sourced.

LightkeeperIQ Education focuses the Lightkeeper experience on early-childhood care and education workforces.

Designed for

  • Childcare and early-learning operators
  • Educator recruiting and workforce teams
  • Economic-development and community partners
  • Training providers and education leaders

Included

  • Public WARN signal screening
  • State market comparisons and activity index
  • Provider search, filters, watchlists, and exports
  • Source links and data freshness

Responsible use

Public notices may be incomplete, delayed, amended, or withdrawn. A matched employer does not establish that every affected worker is an early-childhood educator or childcare worker. Verify details with the original source.
